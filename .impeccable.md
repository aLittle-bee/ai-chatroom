# AI Chatroom — Design Source of Truth

## Design Context

### Users
Francesco (the author) and open-source devs / AI tinkerers who clone it.
Context: long desk sessions — often at night — watching several AI models
argue, with occasional interjections. Reading-heavy: the transcript is
consumed like an article that writes itself. Secondary: phone on the same
network. The job: follow a multi-voice conversation effortlessly, always
know who's speaking and what the room is doing (running / waiting / paused).

### Brand Personality
**Observant. Dry. Precise.** An observation deck for machine conversation —
the room is the instrument, the models are the show. Understated confidence,
zero hype. The owner's broader taste (serious athlete building serious
tools; Whoop/Oura-calibre restraint) applies: no emojis, no playful motion,
nothing cartoonish.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Dark, derived not defaulted**: night reading + terminal adjacency.
  Quiet, deep, slightly blue-tinted surfaces. Light mode is out of scope.
- **Editorial transcript, not chat bubbles**: the conversation reads like a
  typeset debate transcript. Speech is typographically first-class; chrome
  recedes.
- **Two typographic layers**: human-legible speech (Schibsted Grotesk) vs
  machine metadata — model ids, timestamps, counts, settings (Fragment
  Mono). The contrast IS the identity.
- **Voice color is functional**: each participant owns one hue, applied
  sparingly (name + marker only), derived at uniform perceived lightness
  (OKLCH) so no voice shouts.
- **Anti-references**: Discord/Slack clones, generic AI slop (purple
  gradients, glassmorphism, neon glow), dashboard-y card grids, colored
  side-stripes.

### Design Principles
1. **The conversation is the interface.** Every pixel of chrome must earn
   its place against more reading room.
2. **Identity through type, not decoration.** Two families, strict roles;
   color-coded voices; no ornament.
3. **Motion is feedback only** (Emil Kowalski rules): entrances/exits and
   press feedback, under 250ms, custom ease-out, hover-gated on touch,
   honors reduced motion. Never animate the frequent path into slowness.
4. **Every state is designed**: empty room, streaming, waiting-for-host,
   paused-with-reason, provider error.
5. **OKLCH everywhere**; neutrals tinted toward the accent hue (~255);
   accent rare by design (60-30-10).
